      Features

      Made for every reader, styled for every brand.

      • Accessibility modes

        Yellow on black, black on yellow and grayscale schemes, text from 87.5% to 150%, all checked for contrast.

      • Five skins

        Pick any color: Orcanix builds an OKLCH ramp for light, dark and high contrast and keeps text readable.

      FAQ

      Questions, answered.

      Which editions do I get?

      All six editions are available now: HTML, React with Next.js, Vue with Nuxt, Angular, SvelteKit and Astro, with the design tokens and documentation, included in every purchase.

      Do I need to know Arabic?

      No. English is the default. Arabic and right-to-left layouts are ready when you need them, and you can remove the dictionaries if you never will.

      Is it really accessible?

      Every page is checked with axe in light, dark and high contrast, in English and Arabic, with menus and dialogs open, and colors are checked by the build. Automated checks are a floor, not a certificate.

      Can I get a refund?

      Yes, within 14 days if the template does not work as described. See the refund policy for details.

      How do I get updates?

      Updates are free for version 1. Download the latest files from the link in your Lemon Squeezy receipt.
